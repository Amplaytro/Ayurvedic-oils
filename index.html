The method

Heat is the enemy
of a good oil.

Ayurvedic texts repeat a single instruction: taila sneha — anoint with oil. But the modern industry heats, refines and strips. It destroys the very compounds the tradition built a practice around.

So we made the slow way. Botanicals are stone-ground, then steeped in cold-pressed sesame or coconut for twenty-one days. Nothing in our range ever exceeds 40°C. It is slower, it costs more, and it is the entire reason this company exists.

  • 21-day cold infusion. Macerated without solvents, steam or centrifuge.
  • No synthetic fragrance. Scent comes only from the plants listed on the box.
  • GMP-certified batch tested. Every lot carries a published certificate of analysis.

How to use

Abhyanga in four steps.

The classical nightly self-massage. Five minutes a day is the dose the tradition asks for — consistency matters more than quantity.

Warm

Warm a generous amount in your palms until it turns liquid and fragrant. Never heat oil on a stove or in a microwave.

Apply

Work into warm, slightly damp skin using long upward strokes. Always move toward the heart, never downward.

Rest

Leave for fifteen to twenty minutes. This is the snehana — the moment the oil and the skin actually negotiate.

Bathe

Wash with lukewarm water, not hot. Hot water strips the sheen you just spent twenty minutes creating.
